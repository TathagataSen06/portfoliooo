3Description

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at BIT Mesra. This summer I was a Software Development Engineer intern at the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre, building the multithreaded pipeline and peak-detection framework used in detector commissioning. Before that, I won the AI for Bharat Hackathon with SanchaarSetu, middleware that keeps Karnataka's Single Window System in sync with department systems.

Most of my work sits in data paths: getting events, measurements or transactions from where they're produced to where they can be trusted, with retries, idempotency, validation and audit along the way.

Outside work: tennis, football, and anime (One Piece, Attack on Titan, Bleach).
