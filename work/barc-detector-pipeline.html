Application Report

Peak Detection for Detector Commissioning at BARC

Tathagata Sen · Software Development Engineer intern, Bhabha Atomic Research Centre, Mumbai · May–Jul 2026

ABSTRACT

Commissioning a detector meant identifying peaks in its data by hand, dataset by dataset, across thousands of datasets. During my internship I built three pieces for that workflow: a multithreaded Python pipeline that processes 40M+ measurements across 64 channels, a 12-stage peak-detection framework rooted in CERN high-energy-physics methods, and desktop software that automates most of the manual identification. Together they took commissioning from weeks to minutes.
